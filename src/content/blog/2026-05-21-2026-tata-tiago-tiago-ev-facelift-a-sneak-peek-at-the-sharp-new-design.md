Tata Motors has released a fresh teaser for the upcoming Tiago and Tiago EV facelift. The design signals a clear shift from the previous rounded aesthetic to a much sharper, edgy profile. While the official launch is set for **May 28**, the teaser offers a glimpse into the exterior changes and potential safety upgrades.

---

## 🎨 Fresh Exterior: Moving Toward Sharp Edges

The 2026 Tiago appears to have a completely revised front fascia. It features slim, rectangular LED headlamps and a prominent lower air dam. One can observe vertical air vents on the bumper, likely designed to enhance the vehicle's aerodynamics. 

**Key Design Elements:**
- **Grille:** A blanked-out gloss black grille for the petrol version; the EV gets a fully body-colored front end.
- **Wheels:** A new dual-tone alloy wheel design, with aero inserts specifically for the electric version.
- **Rear Profile:** A brand-new connected LED taillamp cluster and a sharkfin antenna have been added for a more premium look.

## 🛋️ Interior and High-Tech Features

While the interior remains mostly under wraps, the teaser suggests a few key updates to the cabin. The hatchback is expected to feature a free-standing digital instrument cluster and updated seating with integrated headrests.

The most notable technology addition appears to be a **360-degree camera system**, which could be a first-in-segment highlight. Other potential features include a larger touchscreen and a wireless charging pad.

[![Tiago 360-Degree Camera Teaser](enhanced_tiago_2_iwcvdq)](https://amzn.to/placeholder)

---

## 🛡️ Safety Upgrades

Safety has been a core focus for Tata. The 2026 facelift is likely to receive a boost with the potential inclusion of **6 airbags**. Other safety tech observed or expected includes:
- Electronic Stability Program (ESP)
- Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS)
- ISOFIX child seat anchors
- Hill Hold Control (HHC)

---

## ⚙️ Powertrain Performance

The petrol Tiago is expected to retain its 1.2-litre engine, possibly available with a factory-fitted dual-cylinder CNG kit. 

| Spec | 1.2L Petrol | 1.2L Petrol + CNG |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Estimated Power** | 86 PS | 73.4 PS (CNG Mode) |
| **Estimated Torque** | 113 Nm | 95 Nm (CNG Mode) |

For the **Tiago EV**, rumors suggest larger battery packs may be introduced, potentially leading to better range figures and faster DC charging times.

[![Tiago EV Charging and Badge](enhanced_tiago_3_n446jo)](https://amzn.to/placeholder)

---

## 🏁 Expected Pricing and Rivals

The official launch on May 28 will confirm the final pricing. Estimates suggest the petrol version could start around **₹4.50 lakh**, while the Tiago EV may start near **₹8 lakh** (ex-showroom).

The Tiago continues to compete with established names like the Maruti Swift, Maruti WagonR, and the Hyundai Grand i10 Nios. The EV variant will be a strong alternative to the MG Comet and Citroen eC3.
