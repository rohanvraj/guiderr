If you have recently searched for your **credit card application status** only to find it was "Declined," you are not alone. In 2026, Indian banks have implemented stricter algorithmic filters to manage risk. 

A rejection doesn't mean you are financially "unsuccessful"; it simply means your current data profile did not meet the bank's specific internal criteria at this moment. Understanding these criteria is the first step toward a successful application in the future.

## **Common Reasons for Rejection**
While every bank has its own "secret sauce" for approval, most rejections from major institutions like SBI or HDFC fall into three categories:

1.  **CIBIL Score Below 750:** For premium cards, a score below 750 is often an automatic filter.
2.  **Job Profile & Office Address:** If you are a freelancer or work for a very small startup, banks may struggle to verify your employment stability.
3.  **Low Credit-to-Debt Ratio:** If you already have several loans or cards, banks may worry about "Over-leveraging."

If you have been rejected due to a low CIBIL score or a lack of salary history, the most logical (and safest) path is an **FD-backed credit card**. This is an honest way to "prove" your reliability to the banking system without needing a salary slip.

## **Managing the "Hard Inquiry" Risk**
Every time you apply for a card and get rejected, your CIBIL score takes a small hit (a "Hard Inquiry"). 
*   **Neutral Advice:** If you have been rejected by SBI or HDFC today, **do not apply for another card immediately.** 
*   **The Wait:** Wait at least 3 to 6 months. Use this time to track your expenses and ensure all your current bills are paid on time.
