The Honda City remains a cornerstone of the Indian mid-size sedan market. On May 22, 2026, Honda Cars India introduced the latest facelift of this iconic model. With an introductory starting price of **₹12 lakh (ex-showroom)**, the 2026 City aims to compete against the likes of the Hyundai Verna and Volkswagen Virtus.

Notably, Honda also used this event to showcase the **ZR-V SUV**, marking its debut in the Indian market.

---

## 🎨 Design Updates: A Sharper Fascia

The 2026 City features a significant visual overhaul. The front profile is now more modern, moving away from the chrome-heavy look of previous years.

**Key Exterior Changes:**
- **Honeycomb Grille:** A new honeycomb pattern is flanked by updated headlamp units.
- **Light Strip:** A sleek light strip now connects the headlamps across the front.
- **Brand Identity:** The new Honda logo is positioned strategically between the hood and the grille.
- **Rear Profile:** One can observe smoked taillights and a cleaner boot lid with an integrated spoiler.

## 🛋️ Interior and Technology Upgrades

Inside, the cabin maintains its premium layout but receives a much-needed tech boost. The primary update is the new **10.25-inch touchscreen infotainment system**, which supports wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay. 

The overall ergonomics remain familiar, ensuring ease of use for long-time Honda owners.
