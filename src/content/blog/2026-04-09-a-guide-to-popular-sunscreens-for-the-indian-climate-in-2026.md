As we approach the peak of summer in **2026**, the intensity of UV radiation in India has made sun protection a non-negotiable part of daily health. Sunscreen is no longer just a "beach essential"; it is a functional shield against premature aging, hyperpigmentation, and long-term skin damage.

## **Why Sunscreen Matters**
The sun emits two types of harmful rays: **UVA** (which causes aging and wrinkles) and **UVB** (which causes sunburns). In 2026, most dermatologists recommend "Broad Spectrum" protection, which shields you from both. Consistent use of sunscreen helps maintain an even skin tone and preserves the skin's natural barrier.

## **How to Choose the Right One**
1.  **SPF Rating:** SPF 50 is generally recommended for the Indian outdoors, blocking about 98% of UVB rays.
2.  **PA Rating:** Look for **PA++++**. The more '+' signs, the higher the protection against UVA rays.
3.  **Skin Type:** Gel-based formulas usually suit oily/acne-prone skin, while cream or ceramide-based formulas are better for dry skin.
