### 🧮 The "Guiderr" Math: Break-Even Analysis

You should always look for the break-even point. The card carries a **₹999 annual fee (+GST)**. To recover this fee through rewards alone, you need to earn at least ₹1,179 in cashback. 

*   **The Break-Even Point:** At a 5% cashback rate, you need to spend approx **₹23,580 online per year** just to cover the cost of the card. 
*   **Fee Waiver:** SBI waives the ₹999 fee if your total annual spend exceeds **₹2,00,000**.
*   **The Reward Cap:** Cashback is capped at **₹5,000 per month**, meaning the 5% benefit applies to your first ₹1,00,000 of monthly online spend.

| Monthly Online Spend | Monthly Cashback | Annual Value (Net of ₹999 Fee) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ₹5,000 | ₹250 | ₹2,001 |
| ₹10,000 | ₹500 | ₹5,001 |
| ₹16,666 (Fee Waived) | ₹833 | ₹10,000 |

### ⚠️ The Guiderr Filter: Who should NOT apply?

To remain neutral, we must highlight those who might find this card unsuitable:
1.  **The Fuel Commuter:** There is **zero cashback on fuel**. You only get a 1% surcharge waiver. If your primary spend is at the pump, this is not the right instrument for your wallet.
2.  **Existing SBI Users:** Please note that eligibility for new card variants can be restricted if you already hold an active SBI Credit Card.
3.  **The "New-to-Credit" Self-Employed:** Per current 2026 eligibility rules, "New-to-Credit" (zero CIBIL history) applicants are generally only approved if they are salaried.

***

### 🔍 Technical Eligibility Audit
Before applying, ensure you meet these specific 2026 criteria to protect your CIBIL score:
*   **Credit Score:** 650+ (with no delayed payments in the last 12 months).
*   **Income Requirement:** Minimum ₹30,000 per month.
*   **The VKYC Rule:** You must be physically located within **50km** of the address on your Aadhaar during the Video KYC process.
