### ⚠️ The Guiderr Filter: Who should NOT apply?

To remain neutral, we must highlight those who might find this card unsuitable:
1.  **Existing SBI Users:** If you currently hold (or have ever held) an SBI Credit Card, you are not eligible for the additional activation profit.
2.  **The Fuel Commuter:** There is **zero cashback on fuel**. You only get a 1% surcharge waiver. If your primary spend is at the pump, this is the wrong tool for you.
3.  **The "New-to-Credit" Self-Employed:** Per current 2026 eligibility rules, "New-to-Credit" applicants are generally only approved if they are salaried. If you have no CIBIL history and are self-employed, wait until you build a score elsewhere.
