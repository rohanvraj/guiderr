## 🧠 Quick Insight: The 'Last Mile' of Ironing

Before you decide, look at your clothes. If 90% of your laundry consists of basic cotton school uniforms or daily-wear tops, a **dry iron** is usually sufficient. However, if you wear thick linens or formal cotton shirts daily, a **steam iron** might be the better "time-saver" because of how quickly it relaxes the fabric.

---

## 🏁 Final Interpretation

- **Go for Steam if:** You want to finish the job faster and have many thick cotton garments.
- **Go for Dry if:** You want an indestructible, low-maintenance tool for a basic daily press.
- **Go for a Steamer if:** You travel often or need to maintain delicate fabrics without a board.
