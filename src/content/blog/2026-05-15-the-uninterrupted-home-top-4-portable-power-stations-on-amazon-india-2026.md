## 🧠 Guiderr Insight: Why LiFePO4 Matters

In the past, power backups used Lead-Acid (heavy and short-lived) or standard Lithium-ion (less stable). In 2026, **LiFePO4** is the gold standard. It is safer, handles heat better, and lasts 6x longer than previous tech. 

If you are an investor or professional, look at the **Cost Per Year**. A high-quality EcoFlow unit might cost more today, but because it lasts 10 years, it is significantly cheaper than replacing a standard UPS every 2 years.
