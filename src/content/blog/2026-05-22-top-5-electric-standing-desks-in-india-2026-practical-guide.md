## ❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Thinking "DIY" is easy:** These desks weigh 30kg to 50kg. If a brand offers "Free Installation" (like Wakefit or Green Soul), take it. Aligning the motor rod perfectly is tricky for beginners.
2.  **Skipping the Anti-Collision:** Always ensure your desk has this. If the desk hits a chair or a drawer while moving down, it can burn out the motor. All desks on this list have this feature.
3.  **Cable Management:** In India, we often have multiple power strips. If your cables are too short, they will pull your laptop or monitor off the desk when you move it to "Standing" mode. Always check your wire length first.
