## ❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Ignoring the Size:** A 3-inch idol might look small in a huge hall but perfect on a desk. Always check the measurements (cms/inches) on the product page before clicking "buy."
2.  **Using Water to Clean:** Silver plating is thin. Using water or soap can cause it to peel or turn black over time. Stick to a soft, dry microfiber cloth.
3.  **Last-Minute Orders:** Many of these items are handcrafted. If you need them for a specific event like Diwali or a wedding date, ensure you check the delivery timeline.
