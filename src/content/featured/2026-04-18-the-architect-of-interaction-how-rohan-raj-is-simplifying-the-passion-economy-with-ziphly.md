In 2026, in an era where "content is king," many creators and professionals find themselves trapped in a paradox: they have thousands of followers but no simple way to trade their time for value. Whether it’s a painter wanting to host a weekend workshop or a doctor offering specialized consultations, the technical barrier to entry has historically been too high.

Enter **Rohan Raj**, an Aston University Finance graduate and a seasoned entrepreneur with 11 years of "boots-on-the-ground" business experience. His latest venture, **Ziphly**, is designed to strip away the complexity of the digital service economy. 

"The goal wasn't to build another complicated social network," says the team at Ziphly. "The goal was to build a bridge."

***

## **1. Beyond the Follower Count**
Ziphly addresses a specific pain point in the Indian market: the transition from "influencer" to "service provider." The platform allows anyone—from musicians to financial consultants—to list and sell 1:1 sessions or group workshops in just a few clicks.

> INSIGHT: **The modern professional doesn't need more likes; they need more 'booked' slots. Ziphly focuses on the transaction of knowledge rather than the vanity of engagement.**

***

## **2. A Tool for Every Vertical**
What makes Ziphly unique is its versatility. It isn't just for "techies." The platform has seen early interest from a diverse range of professionals:
*   **Artists & Musicians:** Booking intimate masterclasses.
*   **Healthcare Professionals:** Managing private consulting hours.
*   **Intellectuals:** Hosting guided conversations on finance, current affairs, or global trends.

![Ziphly Interface Showcase](ziphly-dash_olkxus)

***

## **3. The Math of Transparency**
In a market filled with hidden subscription fees and "platform charges," Ziphly has opted for a clinical, accounting-grade approach to pricing. The model is built on a flat **10% commission** on successful sales. 

> THE BOTTOM LINE: **For a consultant charging ₹2,000 for a 30-minute session, Ziphly’s 10% model means the creator keeps 90% without any upfront monthly overhead. It aligns the platform’s success directly with the creator’s success.**

***

## **4. The Human Element: Guided Conversations**
Beyond business, Ziphly is fostering a new culture of "Meeting with Purpose." The platform encourages users to list "Guided Conversations"—sessions where people meet not just to work, but to exchange high-level ideas or simply meet new people in a structured, safe environment.

> REALITY CHECK: **Digital isolation is at an all-time high. Ziphly uses technology to facilitate real, human-to-human interaction, proving that the internet can be a place for deep connection, not just shallow scrolling.**

***

## **Conclusion: A New Standard for Creators**
By merging the precision of financial logic with a deep understanding of the Indian entrepreneur's hustle, Rohan Raj has positioned Ziphly as a central hub for the 2026 creator economy. It is a lean, powerful instrument for anyone ready to turn their passion into a professional service.
